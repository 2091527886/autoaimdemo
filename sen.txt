 Roblox - ADS (AR)
位置
在游戲中:
Settings -> Game
靈敏度
(最小值 | 預設 | 最大值)
靈敏度1:
Camera Sensitivity 0.001 | 1 | 4
因數1:
Mouse Sensitivity 0.004 | 1 | 4
因數2:
Aim Sensitivity Multiplier 0.004 | 1 | 4
筆記:
The Camera Sensitivity is the Roblox main sensitivity, while the Mouse Sensitivity and Aim Sensitivity Multiplier is the Phantom Forces specific sensitivity.
FOV:
Field of View 60 | 80 | 120
FOV類型:
Vertical Degrees
FOV筆記:
Set the FOV in the game settings.
受影響:
FOV: (Monitor Distance Vertical 0%) | Windows 指標速度:
遊戲引擎:
ROBLOX Rendering Engine
